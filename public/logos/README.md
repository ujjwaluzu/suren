# SUREN logo concepts

Open index.html to compare the three directions, switch palettes, and inspect smaller sizes. Open comparison.png for the complete concept board.

- **01 — Structural S:** Two angular members form a split S. Broad geometric lettering gives it the character of an engineering practice. Folder: 01-structural-s/
- **02 — Connected System:** Four rounded modules connect around a central junction. Open joints and softer lettering suggest an approachable technical partner. Folder: 02-connected-system/
- **03 — Editorial Wordmark:** A compact, custom-drawn masthead with deep counters and cut terminals. No separate emblem: the name is the identity. Folder: 03-editorial-wordmark/

Each folder contains:

- logo-ink.svg / .png — dark ink, transparent background
- logo-cream.svg / .png — warm cream, transparent background
- logo-accent.svg / .png — cream and electric lime, transparent background
- symbol-ink.svg and symbol-cream.svg — square icon canvases
- preview-dark.png and preview-light.png — presentation previews

All logo lettering is drawn as SVG paths; no fonts or external services are needed to render the logos. PNG lockups are 1800px wide. Palette: #0a0a0a / #f1ead6 / #c6ff35. Selected website identity: Structural S / Electric accent.

Rebuild assets with: node scripts/build-logo-concepts.mjs
